Theory & Concept

“Emotion analysis attempts to identify the emotional state expressed in text or speech.”

Academic Notice: This is an educational rule-based demonstration developed for laboratory study. Unlike black-box neural networks, rule-based systems rely on transparent semantic dictionaries, morphological stemming, contextual negation rules, and modifier weightings to systematically score emotional intensity.

Analysis Pipeline

1
Speech / Text Ingestion

Captures vocal utterance via Web Speech API or manual text input.

2
Tokenization & Stemming

Splits raw text into tokens, normalizes case, and stems affixes (e.g. crying → cry).

3
Context & Modifier Evaluation

Scans a 2-word lookback window for intensifiers (extremely × 2.0) and negations (not happy).

4
Confidence & Distribution Normalization

Calculates relative category weights and normalizes distribution to sum strictly to 100%.
